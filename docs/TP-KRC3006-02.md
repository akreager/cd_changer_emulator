## 1. Purpose

This test plan defines the inspection, assembly, and verification procedures for the 13-pin mini DIN protocol sniffer breakout board (DN-KRC3006-04). It covers the period from bare board receipt through final acceptance, at which point the board is released for integration into the protocol capture test bench per TP-KRC3006-01.

## 2. Scope

This plan covers three phases: bare board inspection and testing of the unpopulated PCB, component assembly with workmanship inspection, and final electrical verification of the fully assembled board. It does not cover protocol capture testing, which is addressed in TP-KRC3006-01.

## 5. Safety

All Phase 1 and Phase 2 procedures are performed on the unpowered board with no connection to the head unit, changer, or any voltage source. No specific electrical safety precautions are required beyond standard ESD practices when handling the PCB.

Phase 3 verification step 3.6 involves connecting the breakout board inline between the head unit and changer with bench power applied. Follow the power-down reconfiguration procedure in DN-KRC3006-04 Section 7 before making or changing any connections.

## 6. Phase 1 — Bare Board Inspection and Testing

Phase 1 is performed on the unpopulated PCB as received from fabrication. The purpose is to verify that the board was manufactured correctly before committing components to it. All results are recorded in TL-KRC3006-02, Phase 1.

### 6.1 Visual Inspection

| Step | Procedure | Accept Criteria |
|----|----|----|
| 1.1 | Inspect board outline and corner radii. Compare against DN-KRC3006-04 board dimensions (22 x 80 mm). Measure with calipers. | Dimensions within +/- 0.2 mm of nominal. Corner radii present. No edge delamination or burrs. |
| 1.2 | Remove edge routing burrs. | Any edge burrs trimmed and filed flush. No sharps edges remaining. |
| 1.3 | Inspect right-edge cutouts for J4/J5 audio jacks. Verify cutout geometry is clean and complete. | Cutouts match design. No incomplete cuts or hanging tabs. FR4 intact around cutout perimeter. |
| 1.4 | Inspect all through-holes for J1, J2, J3, J4, J5. Check for blocked, undersized, or missing holes. | All holes open and centered in pads. No drill breakout. Holes accept component leads without forcing. |
| 1.5 | Inspect front (F.Cu) copper traces under magnification. Check for trace breaks, bridging, or missing copper. | No visible trace defects. No bridging between adjacent traces or pads. |
| 1.6 | Inspect back (B.Cu) copper traces under magnification. | Same criteria as step 1.4. |
| 1.7 | Inspect front silkscreen legibility. Verify signal labels (REQ H, GND, B.U., ON, MUTE, D. GND, RESET, CLK, REQ C, DATA C, DATA H), orientation labels (Head Unit Side / Changer Side), and reference designators (J1-J5). | All text legible. No missing or smeared labels. Correct signal names per DN-KRC3006-04 Section 8. |
| 1.8 | Inspect back silkscreen. Verify author, date, revision, and GitHub URL. | All text legible and correct. |
| 1.9 | Inspect solder mask coverage. Check for mask voids, misalignment, or pinholes exposing unintended copper. | Mask intact with no unintended copper exposure. Pad openings correctly aligned. |

### 6.2 Bare Board Electrical Tests

These tests verify that the PCB traces are intact and that no unintended shorts exist between adjacent nets. Perform all tests with the DMM in continuity/resistance mode. The board is unpopulated — test pad-to-pad on the bare copper.

| Step | Procedure | Accept Criteria |
|----|----|----|
| 1.10 | Continuity — J1 to J2 pass-through, digital signals. For each of the following DIN pins, verify continuity between the J1 pad and the corresponding J2 pad: Pin 1 (REQ H), Pin 3 (B.U.), Pin 4 (ON), Pin 5 (MUTE), Pin 6 (D. GND), Pin 7 (RESET), Pin 9 (REQ C), Pin 10 (DATA C), Pin 11 (DATA H), Pin 13 (CLK). Also verify ground pass-through: Pin 2 (GND). | \< 1 ohm for each pin pair (11 measurements total). |
| 1.11 | Continuity — J1 to J3 test header tap. For each J3 pin, verify continuity between the J3 pad and the corresponding J1 DIN pad per the J3 pin mapping in DN-KRC3006-04 Section 8. | \< 1 ohm for each pin pair (11 measurements total). |
| 1.12 | Continuity — J1 audio pins to J4. Verify: J1 Pin 8 (R CH) to J4 Tip pad. J1 Pin 12 (L CH) to J4 Ring pad. J1 Pin 2 (GND) to J4 Sleeve pad. | \< 1 ohm for each (3 measurements). |
| 1.13 | Continuity — J2 audio pins to J5. Verify: J2 Pin 8 (R CH) to J5 Tip pad. J2 Pin 12 (L CH) to J5 Ring pad. J2 Pin 2 (GND) to J5 Sleeve pad. | \< 1 ohm for each (3 measurements). |
| 1.14 | Isolation — Audio path separation. Verify NO continuity between: J4 Tip pad and J5 Tip pad. J4 Ring pad and J5 Ring pad. | Open circuit (\> 1 M ohm) for each pair. Ground (sleeve) pads may show continuity if GND is shared — this is acceptable. |
| 1.15 | Isolation — B.U. to adjacent signals. Verify NO continuity between the B.U. pad (DIN Pin 3 / J3 Pin 3) and every adjacent net: GND (Pin 2), ON (Pin 4), and physically adjacent J3 pins. | Open circuit (\> 1 M ohm) for each pair. |
| 1.16 | Isolation — General short check. Spot-check at least 5 non-connected net pairs across the board for unintended shorts. | Open circuit (\> 1 M ohm) for all checked pairs. |

### 6.3 Phase 1 Completion

If all Phase 1 steps pass, the bare board is accepted for assembly. Record the overall Phase 1 result (PASS/FAIL) in TL-KRC3006-02. If any step fails, document the failure, assess whether it is repairable (e.g., a trace bridge that can be cut), and determine whether to repair or scrap the board. If using one of the three fabricated boards, move to the next board and repeat Phase 1.

## 7. Phase 2 — Assembly and Workmanship

Phase 2 covers soldering all components to the board that passed Phase 1 and inspecting the workmanship. All results are recorded in TL-KRC3006-02, Phase 2.

### 7.1 Assembly Sequence

Solder components in the following order. This sequence places low-profile components first to allow the board to sit flat during assembly of taller components.

| Order | Component | Ref Des | Notes |
|----|----|----|----|
| 1 | 1x11 female pin header (2.54 mm) | J3 | Front side. Verify alignment before soldering all pins — tack one end pin first, check perpendicularity, then solder remaining pins. |
| 2 | 3.5 mm TRS audio jack | J4 | Right edge cutout, head unit side. Ensure jack body seats flush against board edge. |
| 3 | 3.5 mm TRS audio jack | J5 | Right edge cutout, changer side. Same as J4. |
| 4 | 13-pin mini DIN connector | J1 | Front side. Align carefully using pin 1 indicator. Verify all 13 pins plus ground/shield lugs are soldered. |
| 5 | 13-pin mini DIN connector | J2 | Back side. Same care as J1. Verify orientation matches J1 when board is flipped. |

### 7.2 Workmanship Inspection

| Step | Procedure | Accept Criteria |
|----|----|----|
| 2.1 | Inspect all J3 solder joints under magnification. | Smooth, concave fillet on each pin. No cold joints, bridges, or insufficient solder. No flux residue bridging adjacent pins. |
| 2.2 | Inspect J4 and J5 solder joints and mechanical seating. | Jack bodies seated flush. Solder joints complete on all through-hole pins. No cracked solder from mechanical stress. |
| 2.3 | Inspect J1 solder joints (all 13 signal pins plus mounting/ground lugs). | All pins soldered. No bridges between adjacent pins (particularly in the tight DIN pin array). Connector body flush to board. |
| 2.4 | Inspect J2 solder joints (back side, same criteria as J1). | Same as step 2.3. Verify no interference between J2 body and front-side components. |
| 2.5 | Clean board with isopropyl alcohol. Remove all flux residue. Allow to dry completely. | Board clean. No visible flux residue under magnification, particularly between closely-spaced DIN pins. |
| 2.6 | Final visual inspection of assembled board under magnification. | No solder bridges, no damaged traces, no lifted pads. All components secure and correctly oriented. |

### 7.3 Phase 2 Completion

If all Phase 2 steps pass, the assembled board is accepted for final verification. Record the overall Phase 2 result (PASS/FAIL) in TL-KRC3006-02. If solder defects are found, rework the affected joint(s) and re-inspect.

## 8. Phase 3 — Final Electrical Verification

Phase 3 verifies end-to-end electrical function of the fully assembled board. All tests in steps 3.1 through 3.5 are performed with the board unpowered and disconnected from any external equipment. All results are recorded in TL-KRC3006-02, Phase 3.

### 8.1 End-to-End Continuity

| Step | Procedure | Accept Criteria |
|----|----|----|
| 3.1 | End-to-end continuity — Digital pass-through with connectors. Insert a 13-pin mini DIN cable into J1. Probe the cable-side pins at the free end. For each of the 11 digital/control/ground signals, verify continuity from the cable pin through J1, through the board trace, through J2, to the J2 connector pins. If a second cable is available, insert it into J2 and measure cable-end to cable-end. | \< 2 ohm for each signal path (contact resistance of two connectors plus trace). 11 measurements minimum. |
| 3.2 | End-to-end continuity — Digital signals to J3 test header. With the cable still in J1, insert a male jumper wire into each J3 socket one at a time. Verify continuity between the J3 jumper wire and the corresponding cable-end pin. | \< 2 ohm for each (11 measurements). |
| 3.3 | End-to-end continuity — Audio path, head unit side. With cable in J1, insert a 3.5 mm plug into J4. Verify continuity: Cable Pin 8 (R CH) to J4 plug tip. Cable Pin 12 (L CH) to J4 plug ring. Cable Pin 2 (GND) to J4 plug sleeve. | \< 2 ohm for each (3 measurements). |
| 3.4 | End-to-end continuity — Audio path, changer side. With cable in J2, insert a 3.5 mm plug into J5. Same measurements as step 3.3 but on the changer side. | \< 2 ohm for each (3 measurements). |

### 8.2 Isolation Verification

| Step | Procedure | Accept Criteria |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 3.5 | Isolation — Audio path separation (assembled). With nothing plugged into J4 or J5, verify NO continuity between: J4 Tip and J5 Tip (R CH isolation). J4 Ring and J5 Ring (L CH isolation). Then connect a male-to-male 3.5 mm cable between J4 and J5 and verify continuity IS restored: J4 Tip to J5 Tip. J4 Ring to J5 Ring. | Without cable: Open circuit (\> 1 M ohm). <br> With cable: \< 2 ohm. <br> This confirms the separated audio path functions correctly in both states. |

### 8.3 Phase 3 Completion

If all Phase 3 steps pass (3.1 through 3.5 required; 3.6 optional), the board is accepted for integration into the protocol capture test bench. Record the overall Phase 3 result (PASS/FAIL) in TL-KRC3006-02.

## 9. Acceptance and Disposition

The breakout board is accepted when all three phases are recorded as PASS in TL-KRC3006-02. Upon acceptance, the board is released for use in TP-KRC3006-01 protocol capture testing.

If a board fails any phase and cannot be reworked, it is set aside. There are three boards in the fabrication order; repeat the test sequence on the next board.
